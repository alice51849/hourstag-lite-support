Why seeing hours works better than seeing dollars

Prices in a shopping app are easy to ignore. Hours of your own work are not. Every purchase costs hours of your life, and HoursTag Lite puts that number in front of you at the moment you are deciding.

  • Life cost: your take-home value per work hour is the yardstick.
  • Type: every purchase becomes Need, Want or Impulse.
  • Value pattern: complete value insights show where your hours really go.

Seeing the hours turns a vague feeling of overspending into a concrete trade-off you can accept or decline on purpose.

Step by step: mindful spending with HoursTag Lite

  1. Enter your income so HoursTag Lite knows your take-home value per work hour.
  2. Before you buy, type in the price and see how many hours of work it costs.
  3. Tag the purchase honestly as Need, Want or Impulse.
  4. For bigger wishes, set a goal: see the work behind it and track your progress.
  5. Check your value pattern regularly to see whether Impulse purchases are going down.
  6. Back up your data: backups include income, spending, goals and preferences.
