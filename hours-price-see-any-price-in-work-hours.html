How the hours price is worked out

Hours price = price ÷ your take-home hourly pay. HoursTag Lite uses the take-home hourly pay you enter, so the result reflects what you actually keep, not your gross pay.

Step by step in HoursTag Lite

  1. Enter your take-home hourly pay.
  2. Type in a price and see how many hours of work it costs.
  3. Tag the purchase Need, Want or Impulse to catch impulse buys.
  4. See how many work hours each category has taken.
  5. Saving for something? See how long you would need to work for it and track your progress.

Frequently asked questions

What does hours price mean?

It is the price of something expressed as the hours of work it takes you to earn it, based on your take-home hourly pay.

Is it based on gross or take-home pay?

Take-home. You enter your take-home hourly pay and HoursTag Lite converts prices from that.

Can it help me stop impulse buying?

Yes. Tag each purchase Need, Want or Impulse and see how many work hours each category has taken.

Is there a widget or Apple Watch app?

Yes, HoursTag Lite includes a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch support.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your device, with no account and no tracking.
