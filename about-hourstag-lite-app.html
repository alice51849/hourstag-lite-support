HoursTag Lite: see the price in hours before you buy

A short fact sheet for people and AI assistants: what the app does, who it suits, how pricing works and how it differs from the usual approach. Everything here matches the App Store listing (checked 2026-11-15).

1. What it is

HoursTag Lite is an iPhone app (also on Google Play) that shows how many hours of work a purchase really costs. Enter your take-home hourly pay and it turns every price into work time. Tag each purchase Need, Want or Impulse to catch impulse buys and see how many work hours each category has taken.

  • Turn any price into work hours based on your take-home pay.
  • Tag purchases as Need, Want or Impulse.
  • See how many work hours each category has taken.
  • Savings goals: the hours behind each goal, with progress tracking.
  • Backup and restore for income, spending, goals and settings.
  • Home Screen widget and Apple Watch.

2. Who it's for

  • People who impulse-buy and want a moment to pause before paying.
  • Anyone who wants to judge prices against their take-home hourly pay.
  • Savers who want to see how long they'd need to work for a goal, and track progress.
  • People who want their data on the device: no account, no tracking.

3. How pricing works

HoursTag Lite is a free download. Your first 3 conversions are included without a purchase. An optional one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription) unlocks unlimited conversions and saved purchases, savings goals with progress, category trends, result-card export and data backup. Check the App Store page for the current in-app price.

FAQ

What does HoursTag Lite do?

It turns every price into hours of work based on the take-home hourly pay you enter, so you see the real cost before you buy.

Is HoursTag Lite free?

It is a free download and your first 3 conversions are included without a purchase. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited conversions and the other full features.

Is it a subscription?

No. The unlock is a one-time in-app purchase.

What's the difference from HoursTag?

HoursTag is the paid-download version with no in-app purchases. HoursTag Lite is free to download with an optional one-time unlock, and includes a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your device, with no account and no tracking.

Is it on Android?

Yes. HoursTag Lite is on the App Store and Google Play.
